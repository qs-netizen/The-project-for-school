01 / ENERGY MOON

IO

The volcanic powerhouse of the Jovian system.

Why Io matters

  • Extreme tidal heating drives intense volcanic activity.
  • Io moves through Jupiter's powerful magnetic environment.
  • Volcanic material feeds the Io plasma torus.
  • Its electromagnetic environment makes it interesting for power-generation research.

Energy pathway

Plasma environment↓Conducting tether↓Induced DC↓Power conditioning↓Beam / local use
⚠ SCIENCE NOTE

An electrodynamic tether is a proposed technology. The voltage and available extractable power depend strongly on tether length, orbit, conductivity, plasma conditions and circuit design; Io should not be treated as a simple battery.

02 / RESEARCH MOON

CALLISTO

The outer-system laboratory and staging ground.

Research priorities

  • Study ancient cratered terrain and primordial material.
  • Investigate the subsurface ocean with robotic instruments.
  • Develop long-duration human research operations.
  • Support communications and missions beyond Jupiter.

Deployment phases

03 / INDUSTRIAL MOON

GANYMEDE

The proposed industrial and logistics capital.

Resource chain

Ice + regolith↓Extraction↓Water / oxygen / hydrogen↓Fuel + manufacturing↓System-wide logistics

Industrial pillars

  • Water and propellant processing
  • Silicate and mineral processing
  • In-space manufacturing
  • Protected hydroponic agriculture
  • Robotic resource logistics
DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Use local resources wherever practical so settlement growth does not require every kilogram to arrive from Earth.

04 / RESIDENTIAL MOON

EUROPA

A shielded human outpost beside a world-ocean.

Why Europa is scientifically important

  • A global subsurface ocean is thought to exist beneath the ice.
  • Tidal heating can supply internal energy.
  • Rock–water interactions may provide useful chemistry.
  • The ice provides substantial shielding when habitats are placed beneath it.

Residence concept

HAB
SUBSURFACE OCEAN
⚠ HABITATION NOTE

Europa's surface is a severe radiation environment. This concept therefore favors heavily shielded or subsurface habitats rather than exposed surface cities.
